# Imprint

**Published by:** Deutsche Gesellschaft für Internationale Zusammenarbeit (GIZ) GmbH, under the Digital Transformation for Inclusive Entrepreneurship in Ghana (DTEG) programme.

**Implemented by:** GFA Consulting Group GmbH.

**Guide edition:** First Edition, August 2026, covering Phase 1 and Phase 2 of the *User Testing and Support to the Adaptation of Digital Solutions for Informal Entrepreneurs* project, 2023–2026.

**Ongoing maintenance:** The Ghana Hubs Network (GHN) is the intended long-term host of this guide as part of the project's sustainability plan (see [Section 9](09-sustaining.md)).

**Anonymization notice:** To protect participant, intermediary, and solution-provider confidentiality, this edition generalizes and anonymizes examples throughout. Intermediary organizations and the solutions they tested are referred to by consistent reference code rather than by name; see [Section 1.3](01-introduction.md#13-about-the-project-and-the-intermediary-cohort) for the coding scheme.
