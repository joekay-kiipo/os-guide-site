# Contributing to OS Guide

OS Guide is an open-source, community-maintained resource. Contributions are welcome, but response and review times depend on whether an active maintainer is available. Submission does not guarantee inclusion.

## Choose a contribution route

- Use the **Report a correction** issue form for inaccurate, outdated, unclear, or broken content.
- Use the **Share field evidence or a case study** issue form for research findings that confirm, challenge, or extend the guide.
- Select **Edit this page** on the website, or edit a file in `docs/`, to propose a focused content change through a pull request.
- Use the **Volunteer as a maintainer** issue form to offer ongoing stewardship.

Search existing issues and pull requests before starting. For a substantial change, open an issue first so the proposal and evidence can be discussed publicly.

## Evidence standard

New guidance should be practical and grounded in documented field activity. State the relevant region, product type, user group, research method, dates, supporting material, and limitations. Contradictory findings are welcome when their context is clear.

Do not submit private or identifying participant information. Only submit material you are permitted to share under the guide's CC BY 4.0 licence.

## Pull requests

Keep changes focused and explain why they are needed. Minor corrections require a basic accuracy check. New research claims, statistics, case studies, and substantial guidance require supporting evidence and review by someone with relevant subject knowledge before they should be merged.

Automated checks may verify formatting and links, but they do not establish the quality of evidence or replace safeguarding review.

## Project status

The GitHub repository is the public source of truth for contributions and maintenance status. If the project no longer has an active maintainer, contributors may fork and adapt it under CC BY 4.0. The repository may eventually be archived as a read-only record if stewardship cannot be found.
